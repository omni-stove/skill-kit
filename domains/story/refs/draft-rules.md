# Rules for drafting a plot and reporting on it

The report on a plot holds what it must stay consistent with, what of it the reader already has, and where it contradicts a setting or an earlier file.

Where the text needs something the plot leaves open, or the plot cannot hold to the report, a setting, or its own Prohibited and Highlights, write what you judge most likely and note the place; you judge what the plot lacks.

Where the plot gives a passage nothing to move, mark the place instead of filling it, even when that leaves the text short of its length; you judge which passages these are.

The notes hold, in plot order, each note: the Scene, what the plot lacks, the lines of the output file that fill it in and how, and the fixes open to the plot, then each place still marked with the information it lacks; with no note, that the plot held.
