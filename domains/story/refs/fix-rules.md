Treat as a defect only a gap the reader cannot fill or one that invites a misreading. A finding matching a check angle is no reason to edit.

Judge separately whether a finding is right and whether fixing it is worth it; fix a confirmed finding only when it is. Add words only where what the reader gains outweighs what the addition costs the passage: tempo, white space, and what the text keeps hidden.

Where a finding admits more than one fix, leave the text as written and return the options to the author.

After each edit, reread the passages before and after it as a first-time reader, and restore the original where the flow broke.

Report what was fixed, what was left as written and why, and the options returned.
