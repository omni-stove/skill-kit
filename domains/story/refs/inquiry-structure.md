# Inquiry Structure

**A narrative principle that elevates the world's opacity into the subject of the story itself.**
Place "something the reader does not yet know" at the center, and drive the narrative through the design of disclosure order.

## Three Components

1. **Lack** — A meaningful blank is carved into the world (lost memory, an inexplicable phenomenon, a person's true intent, an absent past).
2. **Inquiry** — Movement that transforms perception (observation, dialogue, action, reinterpretation).
3. **Revelation** — A moment when the meaning of already-disclosed information shifts, and the landscape looks different from how it first appeared.

## Design Principles

- **Asymmetry of information**: The gap in knowledge among reader, narrator, and characters is the source of tension.
- **Order of disclosure**: Too early kills momentum; too late leaves the reader behind.
- **Revelation forces reinterpretation**: Ideally, not the addition of new information, but a shift in the meaning of scenes already shown.
