# Japanese Web Novel Narration Guards

- Take beats, tension, and slack only from events, sensation, and the POV's judgment state; where they offer none, stay flat
- Give the reader each fact once, counting everything they have read, what any reader knows, and what follows from either
- Cut whole any narration sentence that hands the reader neither a new fact nor a judgment that changes what the POV does or expects next, or that speaks of the text's course rather than the situation; keep a pause, a sensation, or a silence at a turn or the peak of the Scene, in a sentence of its own
- Recall an earlier event only for such a judgment, pointing to it with a noun phrase and spending the sentence on the judgment
- Say that a thing is absent, did not happen, is not so, or is not quite so only where the reader or the POV expected otherwise, or for a silence kept above; let it stand as its own sentence and the last word on the matter
