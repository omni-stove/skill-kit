# Japanese Web Novel Narration Guards

- Take beats, tension, and slack only from events, sensation, and the POV's judgment state; where they offer none, stay flat
- Give the reader a name, term, or setting fact only where the POV meets or uses it; where several land in one sentence, spread them over sentences, one to each
- Cut whole any narration sentence that hands the reader neither a new fact nor a judgment that changes what the POV does or expects next, or that speaks of the text's course rather than the situation; a fact is not new once the reader has read it, any reader knows it, or it follows from either; keep a pause, a sensation, or a silence at a turn or the peak of the Scene, in a sentence of its own
- Say that a thing is absent, did not happen, is not so, or is not quite so only where the reader or the POV expected otherwise, or for a silence kept above; let it stand as its own sentence and the last word on the matter
- Leave what a line or act implies to the reader; narrate only the POV's response to it
- Show the POV's feeling through what they do, notice, or decide; name it only where no reaction can carry it
