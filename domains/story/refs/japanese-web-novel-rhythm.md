# Japanese Web Novel Rhythm Rules

- Keep at least one unresolved tension open at all times; leave exactly one open at scene end
- Alternate assertion（〜だった）with wavering（〜に違いない）, each on a matter the sentence before it leaves untouched; a wavering sets up a later reversal by events
- After 2–3 dense paragraphs, one sparse paragraph: a plain fact, the next question, or a viewpoint-distance shift
- Take beats, tension, and slack only from events, sensation, and the POV's judgment state; where they offer none, stay flat
- Give the reader each fact once, counting everything they have read — earlier Scenes and earlier chapters included — and what any reader knows: cut whole any narration sentence that hands them neither a new fact nor a judgment that changes what the POV does or expects next, or that speaks of the text's course rather than the situation; where two sentences share content, keep one
- Recall an earlier event, or compare the present with one, only to reach such a judgment; point to the event with a noun phrase and spend the sentence on the judgment
