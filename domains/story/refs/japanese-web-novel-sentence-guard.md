# Japanese Web Novel Sentence Guards

- Keep the source's Highlights word for word through every rule and check
- Never end narration you write on a bare noun
- End on a noun + だった only where the noun is itself the new thing the POV saw, heard, or felt, with a paragraph between any two and max 1 per chapter for a look or voice
- End what the POV concludes now on だ, not だった
- Use a metaphor only where plain prose falls short — max 1 per paragraph, 2 per Scene
- State a gesture or posture once, not as a multi-step procedure
- Describe how a voice or sound sounds only where that tells the reader more than the words or events around it, or is the sensation at a turn or the peak of the Scene
