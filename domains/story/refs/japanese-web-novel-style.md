# Japanese Web Novel Style Rules

- Target ~40–50 chars per narration sentence; split anything significantly over ~80 where its action or object changes, but let prose that flows naturally longer stand
- End sentences with verbs/adjectives/auxiliaries by default: no single-noun sentences
- Follow a line of dialogue with narration only where it adds what the dialogue did not say; cut any that restates or confirms it, taigen-dome or otherwise
- Describe directly by default; use metaphor only where plain prose falls short — max 1 per paragraph, 2 per scene, and never the same conceptual system twice in one scene
- State a gesture or posture once, not as a multi-step procedure
- Put a look or voice inside the sentence that carries the action or speech; max 1 sentence per chapter with one as its predicate noun
- Break runs of 5+ short independent sentences with a subordinate clause
- Open each chapter with phrasing distinct from the previous chapter's
