# Japanese Web Novel Style Rules

- Target ~40–50 chars per narration sentence; split anything significantly over ~80 where its action or object changes, but let prose that flows naturally longer stand
- End sentences on a verb or adjective by default, carrying a thing's kind, a look, or a voice inside the sentence of the action or speech it belongs to; end on a noun + だった only where the noun is itself the new thing the POV saw, heard, or felt — max 1 per paragraph, a paragraph between any two, and max 1 per chapter for a look or voice — and never on a bare noun, keeping any the file already has
- State what the POV concludes or supposes now in the copula だ, action verbs staying past
- Follow a line of dialogue with narration only where it adds what the dialogue did not say; cut any that restates or confirms it
- Describe directly by default; use metaphor only where plain prose falls short — max 1 per paragraph, 2 per scene
- State a gesture or posture once, not as a multi-step procedure
