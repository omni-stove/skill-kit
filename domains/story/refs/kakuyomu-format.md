# Kakuyomu Format Rules

Kakuyomu novels are read on screens: space generously, and place blank lines at meaning seams, not by line count.

- Separate scenes with `*` on its own line; no markdown headings
- Always break the line between dialogue and narration
- Blocks of 1–2 sentences and blocks of 3–4 are both normal; keep every block under 5 sentences

Add a blank line at each of these seams:

- a switch between dialogue and narration
- after 2–3 sentences of narration
- a scene, location, or time change
- a topic or mood change
- before/after an important moment

Keep as one block — no blank line inside:

- a run of dialogue exchanges, even across speakers
- the same speaker's consecutive dialogue
- dialogue plus its attribution (「〜」 followed by 〜と言った)
- dialogue plus a short action by the same speaker
- a one-sentence immediate reaction
