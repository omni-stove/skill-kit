Write the plot body above the first heading and each section under its heading.

Plot body
- Each paragraph describes what happens: actions, decisions, discoveries, situational changes, in 2-4 sentences of ~80-120 chars per line
- Describe dialogue by its intent: × "Let's go" → ○ urges them to set out
- No meta-text: × Silence falls / Here the reader learns X / Tension rises / This works as foreshadowing / This image becomes the final shot

Intent
- Leave it blank unless the author has a clear intent to record; then state in one or two sentences what the scene conveys (intent, theme, or emotional target), not how the prose achieves it: × Plant something only the reader notices / Works as setup and payoff
- Put writing instructions ("write it like X", "don't hint at Y") in Prohibited

Length
- Write only the length of the whole plot on one line, without a bullet, in half-width digits: `12000字` for Japanese or `3000 words` for English, with the number the author gives, otherwise the length you judge its events need

Prohibited
- Leave it blank unless the author names something to rule out; then list each as a bullet: tonal mistakes, plot moves, information not to reveal yet

Highlights
- Copy in only narrative phrasing the author or the source text gave, verbatim, one per line without quotation marks

References
- List as `- @<path>` bullets, one per line with no other text, in the order characters → places → items → world, only the setting files the scene needs beyond what the body says
- Include a character only when their settings shape this plot's development, decisions, or outcome, never because they appear or are named
