Plot body
- Each paragraph describes what happens: actions, decisions, discoveries, situational changes, in 2-4 sentences of ~80-120 chars per line
- Describe dialogue by its intent: ×「行くぞ」→ ○ 出発を促す
- No meta-text: × 沈黙が流れる / ここで読者は○○を知る / 緊張感が高まる / 伏線として機能する / この姿が最後の画になる

プロットの意図
- Leave it blank unless the author has a clear intent to record; then state in one or two sentences what the scene conveys (intent, theme, or emotional target), not how the prose achieves it: × 読者だけが気づく仕込みにする / 予告と実演として働く
- Put writing instructions (「〜のように書く」「〜を匂わせない」) in 禁止事項

禁止事項
- Leave it blank unless the author names something to rule out; then list each as a bullet: tonal mistakes, plot moves, information not to reveal yet

ハイライト
- Copy in only phrasing the author or the source text gave, verbatim: narrative lines under `### 地の文`, one per line without quotation marks; dialogue under `### 会話劇` in 「」, with the speaker when not obvious. Add each subsection only when it has content
