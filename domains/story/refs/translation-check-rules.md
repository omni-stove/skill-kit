# Translation Check Rules

Check an English translation against its Japanese source on these dimensions. Read the English alone before opening the source.

Skip `POV:` lines and 【】 markers entirely.

- English-only: translationese — source syntax showing through, calqued collocations, body-part subjects, filter phrases, monotone sentence shape — and violations of the English prose style rules visible without the source.
- Against the source, in both directions: violations of the fidelity rules or of the English prose style rules visible only against the source — source → translation for what the translation loses or thins, translation → source for what it adds, shifts, or reorders.
- Consistency: renderings matching the glossary's decisions and the other translated files' renderings of recurring terms and motifs.
