# Translation Rules

Layout:

- A source file's translation lives at `en/<same filename>` in the source's directory.
- A directory's source files are its `.txt` and `.md` files in natural sort order, minus the synopsis.
- `en/glossary.md` holds the work-wide decisions: name romanization scheme, honorifics, register, recurring term renderings, style decisions, and a Motifs section — one `語 → rendering` line per motif expected to echo beyond its own file.

Fidelity:

- Translate for meaning and voice.
- Keep every scene break, the order of events, and the intent of each paragraph.
- Render wordplay by effect. Add nothing, omit nothing.
- Resolve subjects and pronouns Japanese leaves implicit.
- Keep `POV:` lines and 【】 markers verbatim.
