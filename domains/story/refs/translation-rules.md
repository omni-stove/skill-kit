# Translation Rules

`en/glossary.md` holds the work-wide style decisions — name romanization scheme, honorifics, register — and the name table: a Japanese expression and its rendering per entry, nothing more.

Where a rendering is uncertain, grep the other sources for the Japanese expression and match the rendering in its paired translation.

Fidelity:

- Translate for meaning and voice.
- Keep every scene break, the order of events, and the intent of each paragraph.
- Add no meaning, drop no meaning.
- Resolve subjects and pronouns Japanese leaves implicit.
- Hold one rendering for each deliberately repeated word or image.
- Keep 【】 markers, `Memo:` lines, and the `Subhead:`, `Lead:` and `Source:` keys verbatim; render the `Subhead:` name as the glossary does, and translate the `Lead:` and `Source:` text.
