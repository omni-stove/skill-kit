# Translation Rules

`en/glossary.md` holds the work-wide style decisions — name romanization scheme, honorifics, register — and the name table.

Where a rendering is uncertain, grep the other sources for the Japanese expression and match the rendering in its paired translation.

Fidelity:

- Translate for meaning and voice.
- Keep every scene break, the order of events, and the intent of each paragraph.
- Render wordplay by effect. Add no meaning, drop no meaning.
- Resolve subjects and pronouns Japanese leaves implicit.
- Keep `POV:` lines and 【】 markers verbatim.
